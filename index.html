Morning Routine

My day begins very early, and I wake up around 6 am, immediately taking a shower. I have low-porosity hair, which means I need to wash it early so it has ample time to dry thoroughly. After my shower, I eat a light breakfast and take a quick scroll through design sketches or project emails. I'd take a bit of time to choose an outfit, but generally it would just be business casual, simple, and comfortable. Though I like to stack up on jewelry, especially my rings. And I'd wear a large coat. After graduating and building my career, I moved to a country with a colder atmosphere. Going out without a coat would be a very terrible decision.

I head to the firm with my brown leather messenger bag with my supplies, a laptop, earphones, papers, and a cup of coffee. The morning air feels calm yet expectant, like a blank canvas waiting to be filled with ideas. My workplace is bright and warm with models, sketches, and other tools to bring our concepts to life. Every morning is a chance to build something meaningful, and a proper environment is essential in achieving that.

Midday Tasks & Projects

By midday, my focus shifts from ideas to action. I meet with my clients, engineers, and co-workers to discuss building plans, needed materials, and structural details. To me, collaboration is key in architecture; it is never a solo pursuit. As a team, we ensure that every design is beautiful and practical.

Most of my time is spent reviewing blueprints, sketching revisions, or utilizing design software such as AutoCAD. On busier days, I visit the actual construction sites to check how the plans are taking shape in real life.

To me, each project feels like a dialogue between imagination and reality. My happiest moments are when I finally see the fruits of my labor, my designs successfully constructed into beautiful buildings that put smiles on people's faces.

A Story from My Dream Job

I actually don't know anyone who's working my dream job, but there is a person who inspired me to take up architecture. It's embarrassing to say this, but he isn't actually a person; he's a character. Kaveh from Genshin Impact is my main inspiration for becoming an architect. When I first saw his character, I could immediately tell that he was incredibly well-written and designed. His entire thing is about how he wants to make sure that art is still valued in his city, which values knowledge more. He takes his time to create the most intricate and beautiful designs, even when he's lacking the time and resources to do so. Kaveh doesn't have a lot of inspiring moments since he's a very reserved person. But to me, just the fact that he's willing to take up so much time, money from his own account, and mental strength just to make structural designs is already very inspiring. And I'm not the only person who's been inspired by him. There's a whole social media community of architects and architecture students who were heavily influenced and inspired by Kaveh. Though he's just a fictional character, his endeavors in-game were able to inspire a lot of people to become architects and leave a very artistic impact on the world.
